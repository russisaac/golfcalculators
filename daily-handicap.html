How it's calculated

Golf Australia's formula for an 18-hole Daily Handicap is ((GA Handicap × Slope Rating ÷ 113) + (Scratch Rating − Par)) × 0.93 × Consistency Factor, rounded to the nearest whole number. The Consistency Factor is 0.9986 for men and boys and 1.0483 for women and girls.

Worked example (illustrative numbers)

These tee ratings are made up to show the maths, so use the ratings on your own scorecard. GA Handicap 14.2, Slope Rating 128, Scratch Rating 71, Par 72.

  1. 14.2 × 128 ÷ 113 = 16.085
  2. Scratch Rating − Par = 71 − 72 = −1, so 16.085 − 1 = 15.085
  3. × 0.93 = 14.029
  4. Men and boys: × 0.9986 = 14.009, which rounds to 14
  5. Women and girls: × 1.0483 = 14.707, which rounds to 15

The calculator keeps full precision until the final rounding. The steps above are shown to 3 decimal places.

Source: Golf Australia, WHS Official reference guide for Australian administrators, Operational Guidance & Supporting Regulations, Edition January 2026, §3H (Daily Handicap formulas and rounding).

Frequently asked questions

What is a Daily Handicap?

It is the number of strokes you receive for one round on a particular course and set of tees. It comes from your GA Handicap, the tees' Slope Rating and Scratch Rating, and Par. Your GA Handicap stays the same, but your Daily Handicap changes with the course and tees.

How is a Daily Handicap calculated in 2026?

((GA Handicap × Slope Rating ÷ 113) + (Scratch Rating − Par)) × 0.93 × Consistency Factor, rounded to the nearest whole number (Golf Australia WHS reference guide, Edition January 2026, §3H). The 0.93 is called the Multiplier.

What is the Consistency Factor?

It is part of the Daily Handicap formula: 0.9986 for men and boys and 1.0483 for women and girls. Golf Australia says it reflects that men and boys tend to score less consistently, and it improves equity in single-gender and gender-neutral events (§3H).

What is the 0.93 Multiplier?

A balancing factor. Skilled golfers are generally more consistent than less-skilled golfers, so without the Multiplier higher handicaps would have a strong advantage in handicap competitions (Golf Australia, §3H).

How do I work out a 9-hole Daily Handicap?

Halve your GA Handicap and use the 9-hole ratings: (((GA Handicap ÷ 2) × 9-hole Slope Rating ÷ 113) + (9-hole Scratch Rating − 9-hole Par)) × 0.93 × Consistency Factor. It is usually about half your 18-hole Daily Handicap (§3H). Choose 9 holes in the calculator.

Where do I find the Slope Rating and Scratch Rating?

They are set for each set of tees, usually printed on the scorecard or on the club's Daily Handicap chart near the first tee. Use the ratings for the tees you are playing (and for 9 holes, the 9-hole ratings). If in doubt, ask the pro shop.

How does rounding work?

The result is rounded to the nearest whole number, and .5 rounds up: 10.5 becomes 11. For plus handicaps .5 rounds towards scratch: +0.5 is scratch and +1.5 is +1, but +1.501 is +2 (Golf Australia, §3H).
