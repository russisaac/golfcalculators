Privacy

Last updated 12 November 2026

Analytics

Some pages on golfcalculators.com use Google Analytics to show us how the site is used. Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser.

What is collected

Google Analytics collects the pages you view, the type of device you use (for example phone or computer, and its browser) and your approximate location (such as your city or region). It does not collect your name or email address. The names and scores you type into a calculator are not sent to Google or to us.

Who processes it

Google processes this data for us, including on servers outside Australia.

How to opt out

You can opt out by installing Google’s opt-out browser add-on, or by blocking cookies in your browser settings.

What stays on your device

Some calculators remember your players, handicaps and scores in your browser’s local storage, so you don’t lose them if you close the page. This stays on your device and is not sent to us or to Google. You can remove it at any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings.
