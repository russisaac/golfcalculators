Privacy

Last updated 24 November 2026

Analytics

golfcalculators.com uses Google Analytics to show us how the site is used. Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser.

We also count a few anonymous events: when a round is settled, when a result is shared, when a shared result is opened, when someone starts their own round from one, when a Handicap Index is worked out, when a Daily Handicap is worked out, when a scramble team handicap is worked out (with only the method: USGA, Ambrose or custom), when match play strokes are worked out (with only the format, such as “four-ball”), when a Nassau is settled (with only “1v1” or “2v2”), when someone signs up for or dismisses the email updates, and when a gear link is clicked. Each event records only which calculator or page it was (for a gear link, also which item, such as “rangefinder”, and which Amazon store), never names, scores or amounts, never a handicap, and never an email address. Shared links carry a short tag saying they came from a share.

What is collected

Google Analytics collects the pages you view, the type of device you use (for example phone or computer, and its browser) and your approximate location (such as your city or region). It does not collect your name or email address. The names and scores you type into a calculator are not sent to Google or to us.

Who processes it

Google processes this data for us, including on servers outside Australia.

How to opt out

You can opt out by installing Google’s opt-out browser add-on, or by blocking cookies in your browser settings.

What stays on your device

Some calculators remember your players, handicaps and scores in your browser’s local storage, so you don’t lose them if you close the page. This stays on your device and is not sent to us or to Google. You can remove it at any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings.

Shared result links carry the names and scores inside the link itself; they are never sent to or stored by us and are stripped from analytics.

Feedback form

If you use the feedback form, we collect what you type (what worked, what didn’t and what you’d want), the page it is about, and your email address only if you choose to give it, so we can reply. Leave out anything you would rather not share.

The form is processed and stored by Netlify, which hosts this site, including on servers outside Australia, and is sent to us. We use it only to improve the calculators and to reply to you, and we don’t add it to any mailing list.

Email updates

If you sign up for email updates (in the footer, or after a result), we collect your email address only, plus which page you signed up from. It is processed and stored by Netlify Forms, which hosts this site, including on servers outside Australia, and is sent to us. We use it only to email you about new calculators, at most once a month, and we never sell or share it. Your address is never put in a link or sent to Google Analytics.

To unsubscribe, reply to any email from us, or email the address under Contact below, and we will remove you.

Affiliate links

We may earn a commission from links on this page and others: the “Gear for this game” links under some results go to Amazon: to Amazon.com.au if your browser’s language is English (Australia) or its time zone is in Australia, and to Amazon.com otherwise (your browser works this out on the page; nothing is stored). If you buy something there we may be paid a small commission, at no extra cost to you. As an Amazon Associate (Amazon.com.au and Amazon.com) we earn from qualifying purchases. Those links are marked as sponsored, open in a new tab, and Amazon’s own privacy notice applies once you are on Amazon. We only count that a gear link was clicked, which item it was and which store (Amazon.com.au or Amazon.com).
