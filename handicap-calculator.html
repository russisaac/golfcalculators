    How it's calculated

    Each round becomes a Gross Differential: (adjusted gross score − Scratch Rating − PCC) × 113 ÷ Slope Rating, rounded to one decimal place. Your GA Handicap (the World Handicap System Handicap Index) is the average of the lowest 8 of your most recent 20 differentials, rounded to one decimal place. With fewer than 20 rounds, a smaller number counts:

    • 3 rounds: the lowest 1, minus 2.0. 4 rounds: the lowest 1, minus 1.0. 5 rounds: the lowest 1.
    • 6 rounds: the average of the lowest 2, minus 1.0. 7 or 8 rounds: the lowest 2.
    • 9 to 11 rounds: the lowest 3. 12 to 14: the lowest 4. 15 or 16: the lowest 5. 17 or 18: the lowest 6. 19: the lowest 7. 20: the lowest 8.

    Worked example

    Round 1: a score of 92 off a Scratch Rating of 71.3 and a Slope Rating of 129, with no PCC. 92 − 71.3 = 20.7, and 20.7 × 113 ÷ 129 = 18.133, so the Gross Differential is 18.1.

    After 8 rounds the differentials are 18.1, 20.8, 16.4, 18.1, 21.6, 17.3, 21.8, 23.6. With 7 or 8 rounds the lowest 2 count: 16.4 and 17.3. Their average is 16.85, which rounds up to:

    GA Handicap estimate 16.9

    Round 9 lowers it if its differential is 16.8 or better, because the lowest 3 of 9 rounds count from then on. Tap Load example at the top to see every round.

    Frequently asked questions

    How is a golf handicap calculated?

    Each round becomes a Gross Differential: (adjusted gross score − Scratch Rating − PCC) × 113 ÷ Slope Rating, to one decimal place. Your GA Handicap is the average of the lowest 8 of your most recent 20 differentials, rounded to one decimal place (Golf Australia WHS guide, January 2026, §3(B) and §3(C)).

    What if I have fewer than 20 rounds?

    You need at least 3. With 3 rounds the lowest counts, minus 2.0; with 4, the lowest minus 1.0; with 5, the lowest; with 6, the lowest 2 averaged, minus 1.0. Then the number counted grows: 2 at 7, 3 at 9, 4 at 12, 5 at 15, 6 at 17, 7 at 19, 8 at 20 (§3(D)).

    Is a GA Handicap the same as a Handicap Index?

    Yes. Golf Australia calls the World Handicap System Handicap Index your GA Handicap. Your Daily Handicap is different: it is the strokes you get on one course and set of tees, worked out from your GA Handicap. Use the Daily Handicap calculator for that.

    What is an adjusted gross score?

    Your gross score with each hole capped at net double bogey: par plus 2 plus any strokes you get on that hole. If you played Stableford, it is Par + Daily Handicap + (36 − your points), which is how Golf Australia works it out (§3(B)).

    Why might my official GA Handicap differ?

    Golf Australia also applies the soft and hard caps on how far it can rise above your Low GA Handicap, exceptional score reductions, the daily PCC and 9-hole score combining. This calculator uses only the rounds you enter, so check the Golf Australia app for your official figure.

    What is the highest handicap?

    54.0. Any calculation above that is capped at 54.0 (Golf Australia WHS guide, §3(E); Rules of Handicapping, Rule 5.3). There is no lower limit: a result below zero is a plus handicap, written with a plus sign, such as +1.2.

    How do I lower my handicap?

    A new round lowers it only if its differential beats one that counts by enough to bring the average down. The calculator shows your next round to beat: the highest differential, and roughly the score at your last course, that would lower your estimate.
