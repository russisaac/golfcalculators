FAQs

What is the 0.93?
0.93 is Golf Australia's standard Daily Handicap multiplier, applied to every player's Playing Handicap calculation since the 2 October 2025 changes.
What is the Consistency Factor?
0.9986 for Men/Boys and 1.0483 for Women/Girls. It was introduced on 2 October 2025.
How does the 9-hole version work?
The GA Handicap is halved inside the formula and the 9-hole Slope, Scratch Rating and Par are used.
How is this different from a GA Handicap?
Your GA Handicap is the index. The Daily Handicap is the number of strokes you receive for a specific course and set of tees on the day.
