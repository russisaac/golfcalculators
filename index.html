How we calculate

Every calculator shows the formula with your own numbers and names its source, so you can check the maths.

  • Daily Handicap: Golf Australia's formula, ((GA Handicap × Slope ÷ 113) + (Scratch − Par)) × 0.93 × Consistency Factor (WHS reference guide for Australia, January 2026, §3H).
  • Handicap Index: the average of your best 8 score differentials from your last 20, per the WHS Rules of Handicapping (R&A and USGA) and Golf Australia's guide.
  • Scramble and Ambrose: the USGA team allowances from the Rules of Handicapping, Appendix C (35/15%, 30/20/10%, 25/20/15/10%), or the traditional Australian Ambrose ÷ 8, rounded once.
  • Stableford: points per hole against your net par, as in the Rules of Golf, Rule 21, with the countback.
  • Skins: lowest score wins each hole, gross or net, with carryovers, then one settle-up showing who pays whom.
