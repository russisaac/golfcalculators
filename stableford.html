How it's calculated

On each hole your handicap strokes come off your score to give a net score, which is then compared with par: net double bogey or worse scores 0 points, net bogey 1, net par 2, net birdie 3 and net eagle 4 (Rules of Golf, Rule 21.1b). Strokes go by stroke index (SI), hardest hole first.

Worked example

One player with a Daily Handicap of 20 plays the first 3 holes of the calculator's built-in card. A handicap of 20 gives one stroke on every hole, plus a second stroke on SI 1 and SI 2.

  • Hole 1 (par 4, SI 5): 1 stroke. A score of 5 is net 4, a net par: 2 points.
  • Hole 2 (par 4, SI 3): 1 stroke. A score of 4 is net 3, a net birdie: 3 points.
  • Hole 3 (par 3, SI 1): 2 strokes. A score of 6 is net 4, a net bogey: 1 point.

Total: 6 points from 3 holes.

Two points a hole for 18 holes is 36, which means you played to your handicap.

Rules source: points from the R&A Rules of Golf, Rule 21.1b. Strokes, plus handicaps and countback from Golf Australia, WHS Official reference guide for Australian administrators, Operational Guidance & Supporting Regulations, Edition January 2026, §10C and §11.

Frequently asked questions

How do you calculate Stableford points with a handicap?

Take your handicap strokes off your score on each hole to get a net score, then compare it with par: net double bogey or worse 0 points, net bogey 1, net par 2, net birdie 3, net eagle 4 (Rules of Golf, Rule 21.1b). Add up the points for every hole.

How many shots do I get on each hole?

Use your Daily Handicap and the stroke index on the card. You get one shot on each hole whose stroke index is at or below your Daily Handicap. Over 18, you get one on every hole plus a second from stroke index 1. A plus handicap gives shots back from stroke index 18 (Golf Australia, §10C).

How does the Stableford countback work?

Golf Australia's default, the Australian Countback Method, compares Stableford points for the last 9 holes, then the last 6, then the last 3, then hole by hole from the 18th. After a shotgun start those are holes 10–18, 13–18 and 16–18 (Golf Australia WHS reference guide, January 2026, §11).

How does Stableford work over 9 holes?

Use your 9-hole Daily Handicap, which is usually about half your 18-hole one. Your shots go to the 9 holes in order of their 18-hole stroke index, lowest first. A 9-hole countback compares the last 6 holes, then the last 3, then hole by hole from the 9th (Golf Australia, §3H and §11).

What is a good Stableford score?

Two points a hole is net par, so 36 points over 18 holes means you played exactly to your handicap. Anything above 36 is better than your handicap, and below 36 is worse. Over 9 holes, 18 points is playing to handicap.
